Reference Manual

Smoker FAQ

Cook times, temperatures, and loading notes for each meat.

🍗 Wings
  • Cook 2 hours at 250°F, then check temp.
  • Minimum internal temperature: 165°F.
  • Temp several large drumsticks.
  • Use 1/2 bag of dry rub per case.
  • ⚠️ Use top racks to cook fastest.
🦃 Turkey Breast
  • Cook 3 hours at 250°F, then check temp.
  • Minimum internal temperature: 165°F.
  • Temp the tallest part of the breast.
  • Cook fat side DOWN (on bottom).
  • ⚠️ Use top racks to cook fastest.
🥓 Ribs & Tips
  • Cook 5 hours total.
  • Start at 250°F and finish at 225°F.
  • Use TODAY'S COOK widget to plan the cooking schedule.
  • Minimum internal temperature: 195°F (Ideal: 200-210°F).
  • Tips do not need to be temped.
  • ⚠️ Turn the dial down to 225°F after wings and turkey are complete.
  • Move big ribs to upper racks whenever space becomes available.
🌭 Hotdogs
  • Smoke for 1 hour at 225°F (or 40 minutes at 250°F).
  • ⚠️ Hotdogs can be smoked any time after the first 2 hours (do not mix with raw meats).
  • Recommendation: Load during the final hour of ribs & tips and remove at the same time.
🥩 Pork Shoulder Butts
  • Cook overnight at 215°F (5:00PM to 9:00AM).
  • Rub all sides with dry rub except the fat side.
  • Cook fat side UP (on top).
  • Use the PORK BUTT BALANCER widget to spread the weight evenly across the racks.
  • ⚠️ Do not use top racks (unless absolutely necessary).
