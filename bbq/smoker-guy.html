Reference Manual

Smoker FAQ

Core times, temperatures, and loading notes for each meat.

🍗 Wings
  • Cook about 2 hours at 250°F.
  • Verify 165°F+ internal temperature.
  • Use 1/2 bag of dry rub per case.
  • Use top racks to cook fastest.
🦃 Turkey Breast
  • Cook about 3 hours at 250°F.
  • Verify 165°F+ internal temperature.
  • Use top racks to cook fastest.
🥓 Ribs & Tips
  • Cook about 5 hours total.
  • Start at 250°F and finish at 225°F.
  • Verify ribs are 195°F+ internal temperature.
  • Tips do not need to be temped.
  • Turn the dial down after wings and turkey are complete.
  • Move ribs to upper racks when space becomes available.
🌭 Hotdogs
  • Cook 1 hour at 225°F.
  • Load during the final hour of ribs and tips.
  • Remove at the same time as ribs and tips.
🥩 Pork Shoulder Butts
  • Cook overnight at 215°F.
  • Do not use top racks.
